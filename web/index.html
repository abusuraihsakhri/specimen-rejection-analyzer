How metrics are calculated

The rejection rate is rejected specimens / all valid specimens × 100 when a status column identifies accepted and rejected specimens. The Pareto denominator is rejected specimens only.

Reasons are grouped case-insensitively, after whitespace normalization. Blank reasons are reported as “Unspecified”. Monthly bars show counts, not rates; records without valid ISO dates are omitted from that chart.

Descriptive quality-control reporting only. No statistical significance or CAP/CLSI certification is implied.
